# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One collector (Manuel) and his own Osta playthroughs. He checks the site equally on his PC (often with the game open beside it) and on his phone away from the game, to see what he owns, what is missing, what he can assemble now, and what needs repair.

## Product Purpose

A personal archive of the militaria collection kept in three saved houses (Estonia, Francia, Afganistán) inside the video game Osta. It turns the save files into a browsable record: every object with its exact mounted parts, condition, recommended display house and section, model date and history, plus collection goals (variants), what can be built from spares, and open identification doubts. Success: in seconds he knows what he has, what he lacks and what to do next, and the collection looks worthy of being shown.

## Positioning

The data is read directly from the game's save files and rebuilt automatically when the game opens and closes; variants are checked per exact part (stock, liner, projectile), and anything uncertain is shown as a doubt rather than invented.

## Operating Context

- Static site on GitHub Pages (`docs/`), data in `docs/data.json` regenerated by `pipeline/update.py`; plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.
- Eight sections, in this order: Vista general, Mi colección, Puedes montar, Me falta, Catálogo del juego, Pendientes, Fotos y dudas, Datos y actualizaciones.
- Spanish UI. ~1500 inventory rows, 780 catalogue models, ~1800 variant goals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Filtering, search, sorting, paging, CSV export, detail sheets per object/goal/model, auto-reload of new revisions.
- Doubts (`meta.doubts`) and alerts (`meta.alerts`) must stay visible.
- Real images available: 15 in-game captures in `docs/media/*.webp` (reviewed pieces). No other imagery exists; no product claims beyond the data.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Osta · Archivo de la colección".
- The user wants the game's atmosphere to come through (it should feel like Osta, not a generic admin panel).
- Militaria subject: no militarist, Nazi-era or supremacist emblems as decoration; such symbols appear only as documented facts in data.

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/data.json` (real data), `docs/media/*.webp` (real in-game captures). No testimonials or external proof; none to be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The save file is the truth; never show more certainty than the data has.
2. Fast answers first: what I have, what I lack, what I can build.
3. The collection deserves to look like a collection, not a spreadsheet.
4. Doubts are visible work, not hidden errors.
