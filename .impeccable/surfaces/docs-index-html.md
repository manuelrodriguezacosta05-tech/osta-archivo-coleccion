## Direction contract

THESIS: The collection is a three-wing museum standing in Osta's Estonian forest, signposted like a real gallery. Refuses the generic white admin dashboard with blue accent and stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Spruce-green shell rail (#1d3529), birch-white ground (#eceae2) with paper panels (#f6f5f0), peat ink (#1b231d). House colours carry everything house-related: Estonia spruce, Francia northern-dusk blue (#2f4b66), Afganistán resin amber (#c08a2c); rust (#a3432f) only for repair/doubt stamps. Barlow Semi Condensed for gallery signage (titles, numbers, labels), Barlow for reading. 1px seams, no shadows, small radii. Raises: menu items carry an exact-length extent bar (acetate tab rail); every drawn length is an exact count (labanotation); states are stamped, never hidden (ticket wallet); hairline seams (console); hierarchy by scale contrast (specimen).

STORY: He sees at once how big the collection is, how each house's rooms are filled, and the next step (what he can build, what needs repair, open doubts); then works in dense, filterable lists that wear the same signage.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left spruce rail with eight sections and extent bars. Main: kicker + "La colección, sala por sala", revision stamp at right. Four-figure strip in one seamed band. Below, the floor plan: three wings side by side, each a grid of rooms (sections) whose fill height is the share of displayed pieces; clicking a room opens that house+section in Mi colección.
